# Templates

Fill-in templates for the team's working documents. Copy a template, rename the copy for your client or company, and replace every [bracket] and italic prompt. Keep the originals blank so the next person can reuse them.

| Template | Use it for | Course module |
|---|---|---|
| [`client-profile.md`](client-profile.md) | Pulling every goal, constraint and quote out of the case file, and rating willingness, ability and need | [Module 10](../course/10-know-your-client.md) |
| [`ips-template.md`](ips-template.md) | Drafting the Investment Policy Statement (due November 6) | [Module 11](../course/11-investment-policy-statement.md) |
| [`company-tear-sheet.md`](company-tear-sheet.md) | Comparing stock candidates side by side, one page each | [Module 4](../course/04-financial-statements-and-business-analytics.md) |
| [`investment-thesis.md`](investment-thesis.md) | Making the case for each individual stock, including when to sell | [Module 13](../course/13-portfolio-construction-and-implementation.md) |
| [`trade-log.csv`](trade-log.csv) | Recording every trade and its reason, for the Trading Notes Analysis (due October 23) and the final report | [Module 13](../course/13-portfolio-construction-and-implementation.md) |

Work through them in that order: client profile, IPS, tear sheets, theses, then the trade log as you trade.

To use the trade log, import `trade-log.csv` into Google Sheets or open it in Excel, delete the example row, and log each trade on the day you make it.

Whatever you write in these templates goes into your submissions, so it must be your own work under the competition's AI rule (see the [main README](../README.md)).
