# Private Wealth & Portfolio Management: The Complete Course

Wharton Investment Competition edition · Prepared by Carlos · October 6, 2026

A full course for the 2026–27 Wharton Global High School Investment Competition, built around a conservative, risk-averse client. It runs the private-banking chain end to end: understand the client, write the Investment Policy Statement (IPS), build the portfolio, manage its risk and defend it to judges.

Read it one module at a time below, or as a single [PDF](../pdf/course.pdf). Every formula is also in the [formula booklet](../booklet/formulas.md), and every acronym in the [key terms](../booklet/key-terms.md).
